# Citrobacter freundii resistance dashboard

## Context

The *C. freundii* analysis is complete across every layer: 66,005 resistance islands over 6,061
genomes, three cluster sets (411 resistance, 328 stress, 67 virulence) each carrying per-position
variation, a predicted structure, a Pfam domain architecture and a Foldseek fold match, and Pfam
coverage of all 208,757 pangenome clusters.

All of it lives in TSV tables, 1,261 static figures and 808 per-structure HTML pages. Nothing ties
them together, so answering one question about one gene family means opening five folders. This
dashboard is the layer that makes the work usable and shareable.

The user set the model: island layout and data organisation after the ArchaeaHQ DefenseFinder
dashboard, per-entity presentation after its candidates tab, and visual design from the Simple brand
book.

## Decisions taken

| question | decision |
|---|---|
| cluster scope | The 806 analysed clusters get full detail. The other ~208,000 stay searchable with product, prevalence and Pfam architecture. |
| delivery | Three pages sharing one build script: islands, clusters, structures. |
| islands | Tier 1 and tier 2 only, 46,921 islands. The 19,084 backbone-only controls are dropped. |
| structures | Inline packed CA traces drawn on canvas. No dependency on the 1.2 GB views folder. |

Because the pages are separate files with no server, cross-page links carry state in the query
string, and data two pages both need is duplicated in each. The reference has the same constraint.

## Corrections to assumptions, found by measurement

These change the build and are worth reading before starting.

- **Cluster and island ids are dense, gapless integer ranges.** `CFR-000001`..`CFR-208757` and
  `CFRI000001`..`CFRI066005`. No payload stores them as strings; store the integer and format it
  client-side. This is the single largest saving in the plan.
- **Islands average 7.46 genes, not 10.5.** That figure was tier 1 alone. Tier 1 and tier 2 together
  are 46,921 islands and 350,064 genes.
- **There are 808 drawable chains, not 806.** ClbB has no whole-chain PDB; it has three module files
  and a parent row marked `folded_by_domain`.
- **DuckDB is not installed**, contrary to what I assumed when briefing the design. It is not needed:
  `island_gene_table.tsv` reads in 3.6 seconds and 2.1 GB with plain pandas.
- **Two different "has variation" flags exist and must not be conflated.** 530 of 806 clusters have a
  computed variation summary, but only 439 of 808 structures have any non-zero variation B-factor.
- **Over 40% of clusters are fragments** (334 of 806), so the UI must badge them or readers will
  compare fragment confidence against full-length structures as if equivalent.

## Payloads

New scripts in `latest_snps/scripts/`, continuing the 01–29 convention, all taking `--group` so the
other four organism groups work later. Output to `Citrobacter_freundii/Dashboard/`, mirroring how
`Islands/` and `Structures/` sit beside each other.

| step | script | produces | size |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | `30_dashboard_common.py` | shared base64-varint codec, dictionary encoder, id helpers | — |
| 31 | `31_cluster_payload.py` | `clusters.json`, `cluster_index.json` | 0.4 MB, 3.9 MB |
| 32 | `32_island_payload.py` | `islands.json` | 4.5–5.5 MB |
| 33 | `33_island_genemap_payload.py` | `genemaps.json` | 3.1 MB |
| 34 | `34_structure_payload.py` | `structures.json` | 1.7–2.0 MB |
| 35 | `35_build_pages.py` | the three HTML pages | ~14–17 MB total |

Plus `run_dashboard.py` beside `run_all.py` and `run_islands.py`, which asserts its upstream tables
exist and names the step to rerun if one is missing. `amr_common.py` gains one constant, `DASHBOARD`.
These scripts do not use the `--set` machinery, because they merge all three sets into one payload.

**Encoding**, following the reference's three techniques:
1. One `dicts` object per payload; every repeated string becomes an integer index.
2. Positional arrays with one `fields[]` list, never repeated keys. At 46,921 rows, repeated keys
   alone would cost about 1 MB.
3. Base64-varint packing for the two high-cardinality layers, gene maps and structure coordinates.

**Gene maps**, measured against the real filtered table: per gene a zigzag varint gap from the
previous gene's end, a width, a combined cluster-and-strand integer, and one character holding the
draw role plus a label-override bit. 87.6% of clusters have one label everywhere, so labels are
derived from a per-cluster default and only 18.7% of genes carry an explicit one. Result: 2.6 M
characters, about 3.1 MB with its dictionaries, against roughly 7.3 MB unpacked.

**Structures**: sequence as one character per residue, coordinates delta-encoded at 0.1 Å through the
same varint codec, confidence and variation as ASCII quality strings at one character per residue.
210,971 CA residues across 808 chains come to about 1.55 MB, against 3.15 MB unpacked. Skip the
variation string entirely for the 369 chains that are all zero.

**The second tier** is a structure-of-arrays over all 208,757 clusters: product and architecture
dictionaries (11,514 and 9,878 distinct strings) plus five integer arrays. Measured 3.89 MB, so no
prefix index or island-adjacency restriction is needed. Search is built client-side over the ~21,000
dictionary strings, not precomputed.

## Pages

All three share the Simple shell: JetBrains Mono throughout, the token block verbatim, no radius, no
shadow, no gradient, and the `.frame` component with its `[BRACKETED_LABEL]` cutting the top border.

**`islands.html`** — three tabs, `[ISLANDS]`, `[DISTRIBUTION]`, `[HOW TO READ]`. The islands tab is
the reference's bench grid, `264px | minmax(0,1fr) | 320px`, collapsing to two columns under 1280px
and one under 880px. Left rail: search, species and lineage selects, tier filter, then a capped
scrolling list. Centre: a fit-or-detail toggle, a metadata strip, the gene-map SVG in a scrollable
box, then the legend. Right: the record panel for the selection.

**`clusters.html`** — the most important page. A segmented set filter, `[ALL] [AMR·411]
[STRESS·328] [VIRULENCE·67]`, a sortable sticky-header table, and the detail record docked below in a
`columns:4 300px` masonry. Sections in fixed order: header with badges and actions, identity and
product, anchor tier, per-position variation, structure, Pfam architecture, best fold match, islands
containing the cluster. **Any section whose data is absent is dropped from the DOM, not shown empty**,
so the masonry reflows. ClbB renders its three modules as segments rather than one chain.

**`structures.html`** — bench grid `250px | minmax(0,1fr) | 336px`. Left a cluster rail, centre the
canvas stage with a confidence-or-variation toggle and the per-residue ribbon beneath it, right the
evidence column with the domain strip and the ranked Foldseek hits.

## Gene colours

The existing role convention must survive the move to five accents. Roles keep identity through a
solid stroke; fills are tints capped at 30% so an arrow 15px wide still reads as a shape.

| role | accent | treatment |
|---|---|---|
| acquired resistance | coral | 32% tint, 1.5px stroke |
| chromosomal backbone | coral | 12% tint, 1px stroke |
| point mutation | coral | no fill, 1.5px stroke, centre dot |
| stress | teal | 30% tint, 1px stroke |
| virulence | purple | 30% tint, 1px stroke |
| mobile element | mustard | 30% tint, 1px stroke |
| mobile element, pseudogene | mustard | 10% tint, dashed stroke |
| other | grey | `--bg-2` fill, `--border` stroke |

Green is deliberately excluded from gene roles and reserved for pass and conserved states, so the
legend cannot be misread as having eight categories.

**Two conflicts between the references, resolved.** The brand book forbids colour as fill; a
pure-outline gene arrow is illegible at small widths. Tint plus solid stroke is the compromise, with
identity always carried by the stroke. And AlphaFold's canonical confidence colours are replaced by
the Simple accents for consistency across the dashboard, with the bucket thresholds captioned so
domain readers are not misled.

## Verification

1. Run `run_dashboard.py` and confirm five payloads and three pages are written, with the sizes above.
   A payload more than 20% off its measured estimate means an encoding bug.
2. Round-trip the codecs: decode `genemaps.json` for 100 random islands in Python and check gene
   count, coordinates and roles against `island_gene_table.tsv`; decode 20 structures and check the
   residue count equals `aa_len` and the coordinates match the PDB to 0.1 Å.
3. Open each page from `file://` and confirm it needs no network beyond the Google Fonts link.
4. Spot-check known biology: `blaCMY` should be present at high prevalence with a beta-lactamase fold
   match; `CFRI046929` should show the class 1 integron with its pseudogene transposase; `gyrA`
   should mark position 83 as a catalog site; ClbB should render as three modules.
5. Confirm the degenerate cases render: a cluster with no variation drops that section, a fragment is
   badged, the six ColabFold structures are badged, and a tier-3 island is nowhere reachable.
6. Check the row caps hold by filtering to a single species and confirming the cap resets.
7. Keyboard-only pass over tabs, rail and table, plus a reduced-motion check that spin is off.
