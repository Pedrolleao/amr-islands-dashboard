AMR islands dashboard

Antimicrobial resistance, stress and virulence genes across 7,287 Citrobacter freundii group isolates (NCBI Pathogen Detection PDG000000039.1314). Each page is self-contained and may take a few seconds to load.

  • Islands

    Where each resistance gene sits: 46,921 islands with gene maps, architecture types and aligned views per family or antibiotic class.

  • Gene families

    806 analysed families with predicted folds, per-position variation, Pfam domains and Foldseek matches; the whole pangenome searchable.

  • Antibiograms

    Measured susceptibility for 75 isolates beside their resistance genotype, per antibiotic and per isolate.

Pipeline, documentation and build scripts: github.com/Pedrolleao/amr-islands-dashboard
